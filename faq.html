Identification

ID & Documents

A current government-issued photo ID issued within the last five years. Acceptable forms include: California driver's license or DMV-issued ID, U.S. passport or passport card, foreign passport, U.S. military ID, valid consular identification document, tribal identification card from a federally recognized government, or any other ID satisfying California Civil Code § 1185. Temporary licenses and temporary IDs are not acceptable under any circumstances.

Booking

Scheduling & Availability

Visit our How to Book page to schedule your appointment online. Select the appointment type that fits your needs and pick a date and time that works for you.
Appointments are available Monday through Friday after 6:00 PM, and Saturdays and Sundays starting at 9:00 AM. Late-night appointments (until 12:00 AM) are available any day. Weekday daytime appointments (before 6 PM) are generally not available.
Cancellations with at least 24 hours' notice are free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to a $35.00 cancellation fee. No-shows are charged a $35.00 no-show fee.
The Notary will attempt to contact you by phone and text at the 10-minute and 15-minute marks past the scheduled time. If the appointment still cannot begin after a brief grace period, it will be treated as a no-show and a $35.00 no-show fee applies.
Pricing

Fees & Payment

All notarial acts are a flat $15.00 per act — the California maximum per Government Code § 8211. Travel fees are based on distance from Fremont: $50.00 up to 15 miles, $65.00 for 15–25 miles, and $65.00 plus mileage at the IRS standard rate beyond 25 miles. Urgent or emergency appointments (less than 2 hours' notice) are a flat $100.00 travel fee instead. See the full fee schedule on our Services & Fees page.
Cash, Venmo, Zelle, Apple Cash, PayPal, or Debit/Credit card (subject to an additional $1.50 fee). Payment is due in full at the time of service. A $35.00 fee applies to any failed or reversed digital payments.
No — there are no time-of-day fees. The travel fee is the same whether your appointment is on a weekday evening, a weekend, or a holiday. The only exception is urgent/emergency appointments (less than 2 hours' notice), which are a flat $100.00 travel fee instead of the standard distance-based rate.
Parking, toll, or access fees of $10.00 or less are included in the travel fee at no extra charge. Any fees anticipated to exceed $10.00 will be discussed and approved with the Client before being incurred. If the Client declines, the Notary may cancel the appointment, and the travel fee still applies.
