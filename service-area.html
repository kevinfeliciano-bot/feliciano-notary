Where We Go

Service Area

Mobile notary services within 15 driving miles of Fremont City Hall — Southern Alameda and Northern Santa Clara Counties.

Coverage

15-Mile Radius from Fremont

All appointments within 15 driving miles of Fremont City Hall (3300 Capitol Ave, Fremont, CA 94538) are covered by the standard $50.00 travel fee.

Our Extended Service Area on the Peninsula — Mountain View, Menlo Park, Palo Alto, and Redwood City — falls in the 15–25 mile range and is covered by a flat $65.00 travel fee, the same as anywhere else in that distance range.

For appointments beyond 25 miles, additional mileage is billed on top of the $65.00 base fee at the current IRS standard mileage rate ($0.725/mile). Out-of-area appointments are subject to availability. Urgent or emergency appointments (less than 2 hours' notice) are a flat $100.00 travel fee instead of the standard distance-based rate.

Travel Fee Summary
Up to 15 miles $50.00
15–25 miles $65.00
Beyond 25 miles (per extra mile) $0.725
Urgent / emergency (<2 hrs notice) $100.00 flat
