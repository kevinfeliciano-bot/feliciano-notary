Services & Fees

Clear, published pricing — no surprises. All notarial acts are a flat $15.00 per act — the California maximum under Government Code § 8211.

Notarial Acts

What We Notarize

Each signature line requiring a notarial certificate counts as one notarial act. The Client is responsible for knowing which act their document requires. If unsure, consult the document's issuing authority or a licensed California attorney.

Acknowledgment

The signer personally appears and declares they signed the document voluntarily and for its stated purpose. Common for deeds and powers of attorney.

$15.00 per act

Jurat

The signer personally appears, signs in the Notary's presence, and swears or affirms under oath that the document's contents are true. Common for affidavits.

$15.00 per act

Certified Copy of Power of Attorney

The Notary certifies that a copy of a Power of Attorney is a true and correct copy of the original, as permitted under California law.

$15.00 per act

Copy Certification by Document Custodian

The document custodian swears under oath that a copy is a true and accurate reproduction of the original. Used for documents the Notary cannot certify directly — the Notary administers the oath and notarizes the custodian's signature.

$15.00 per act

Loan Signing Services

Full loan document signings for real estate transactions, refinances, and HELOCs. Trained and Certified Loan Signing Agent. Billed per notarial act, same flat rate as any other session.

$15.00 per act

Oath & Affirmation

Administering oaths and affirmations as required for legal proceedings or official purposes.

$15.00 per act

Multi-Act Sessions

All notarial acts are a flat $15.00 per act regardless of session size — no volume discount on the act fee itself. Travel fees decrease as your session grows and are waived entirely for 10+ acts; time-of-day fees apply to every appointment regardless of session size — see the travel fee schedule below.

$15.00 per act (flat rate)

Travel

Travel Fee

The travel fee is based on the total number of notarial acts in your appointment. All appointments are within 15 driving miles of Fremont City Hall (3300 Capitol Ave, Fremont, CA 94538) unless otherwise arranged.

Acts in Session Travel Fee
1 – 2 acts$45.00
3 – 4 acts$30.00
5 – 9 acts$15.00
10+ actsWaived

For appointments beyond 15 miles, additional mileage is charged at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate (currently $0.725/mile). Time-of-day fees apply to all appointments regardless of session size.

Any parking, tolls, or access fees exceeding $10.00 will be discussed with the Client before being incurred.
