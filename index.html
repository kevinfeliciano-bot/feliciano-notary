What We Offer

Notarial Services

Whether you need an Acknowledgment on a deed or power of attorney, a Jurat for an affidavit or sworn statement, or a Copy Certification by Document Custodian for records that can't be certified directly — Kevin comes to you.

Available at your home, office, a coffee shop, or anywhere that works. All notarial acts are billed at a flat $15.00 per act.

The Client is responsible for knowing which notarial act their document requires. If unsure, consult the document's issuing authority or a licensed California attorney.

Transparent Pricing

No Surprises

All notarial acts are billed at a flat rate of $15.00 per act. The more notarizations in a single session, the lower your travel fee — and for five or more acts, both travel and time-of-day fees are waived entirely.

Where an appointment falls into more than one fee category, the higher rate prevails — fees are never stacked or combined.
