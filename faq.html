Identification

ID & Documents

A current government-issued photo ID issued within the last five years. Temporary licenses and temporary IDs are not acceptable under any circumstances.

Booking

Scheduling & Availability

Visit our How to Book page to schedule your appointment online. Select the appointment type that fits your needs and pick a date and time that works for you.
Appointments are available Monday through Friday after 6:00 PM, and Saturdays and Sundays starting at 9:00 AM. Late-night appointments (until 12:00 AM) are available any day. Weekday daytime appointments (before 6 PM) are generally not available.
Cancellations with at least 24 hours' notice are free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to a $35.00 cancellation fee. No-shows are charged the full travel fee.
The Notary will wait up to 15 minutes past the scheduled appointment time. If all signers are not present and ready within 15 minutes, the appointment will be treated as a no-show and the travel fee will apply.
Pricing

Fees & Payment

All notarial acts are a flat $15.00 per act — the California maximum per Government Code § 8211. Travel fees are $45.00 (1–2 acts), $30.00 (3–4 acts), $15.00 (5–9 acts), and waived for 10+ acts. Time-of-day fees apply to all appointments: +$15.00 weekends 9 AM–6 PM, +$25.00 evenings 6–9 PM, +$40.00 late night 9 PM–12 AM, +$65.00 for less than 2 hours\' notice.
