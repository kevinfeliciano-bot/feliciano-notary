How fees work
✦ Notarial acts: $15.00 per act (flat rate, all sessions)
✦ Travel fee: $50 (up to 15 mi)  ·  $65 (15–25 mi)  ·  $65 + mileage (25+ mi, IRS rate beyond 25)
✦ Urgent/Emergency (<2 hrs notice): $100 flat travel fee (replaces standard travel fee; mileage still applies beyond 25 mi)
✦ Cancellation: free with 24+ hrs notice  ·  $35.00 if less than 24 hrs
